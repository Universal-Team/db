A modern weather app for the New 3DS XL. The top screen is an animated **stereoscopic 3D** scene of the current conditions - drifting clouds, rain, snow and storm lightning - and the bottom screen is the full forecast: a 7-day strip, hour-by-hour detail, city search and Celsius/Fahrenheit toggle.

* Top screen: live 3D weather scene (rain / snow / storm / sun / stars)
* Bottom screen: current conditions + 7-day + hourly forecast
* City search with an on-screen keyboard
* Alerts derived from the forecast (wind, storms, heat)
* Settings and location save to the SD card
* Data from [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com), no account needed
